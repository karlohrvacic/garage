Delete your Garage account

This page explains how to delete your account and its data in Garage (the household vehicle-upkeep app by garage.hrva.cc), as available on Google Play and at garage.hrva.cc.

Delete your account in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Garage and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

Or request deletion by email

If you can no longer access the app, email garage@hrva.cc from the email address of your account and ask for deletion. We will confirm and delete the account.

Delete some of your data without deleting your account

You can delete parts of your data in Garage at any time, keeping your account:

  • Delete individual fuel entries or service records from their lists.
  • Delete a whole vehicle, which removes its entire fuel and maintenance history.
  • Remove an optional vehicle photo by editing the vehicle.
  • Leave a household from Settings to disconnect from its shared data.

These deletions take effect immediately. You can also email garage@hrva.cc to request deletion of specific data.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address and display name) — deleted immediately.
  • Your household membership — removed immediately.
  • If you are the last member of a household, that household's vehicles and their entire fuel and maintenance history are deleted along with it.
  • If other members remain in the household, the household's shared vehicle data (vehicles, fuel entries, service history) stays with them — it belongs to the household, not to a single account.

Retention

Deletion is immediate; no additional copies are kept beyond standard short-lived database backups, which expire automatically. There is no extra retention period.

Before deleting, you can export all your data as CSV from Settings → Export as CSV. For full details, see the privacy policy.
