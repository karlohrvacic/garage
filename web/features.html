Garage

Every vehicle in the garage in one place — fuel, servicing, costs, and what falls due next — shared live with everyone who drives them, rather than tied to one phone.

Open the app

Fuel that tells you something

  • Real full-tank economy, computed from complete tank-to-tank spans — not an average of whatever was typed in. Partial fills and missed fills are handled rather than quietly wrong.
  • Enter any two of volume, price per unit and total; the third is worked out as you type.
  • A car that runs on two fuels gets a separate figure for each.

Maintenance that projects from real use

  • Intervals by distance, by time, or whichever comes first — projected from how the car is actually driven, using every odometer reading the garage has, not just fill-ups.
  • A planner that spots items falling due close together and suggests doing them in one shop visit.
  • Reminders a month and a week ahead, and at 500 km to go.

Money, both directions

  • Insurance, registration, tyres, tolls, vignettes and the rest by category — with the recurring ones reminding you before they fall due again.
  • Income too: lift shares, refunds, and what a car sold for, so "what has this cost me" can have a real answer.
  • Running cost per kilometre, and a settlement view of who has paid what into the shared vehicles.

Croatian fuel prices

  • Live prices from the Ministry's open data, sorted by distance when you allow location.
  • The nearest, the cheapest, and — the one that matters — the cheapest once the fuel to get there is paid for.

Shared, and yours to leave

  • Invite the people who drive the car; everything updates live on their devices too.
  • Belong to more than one garage, and hand a vehicle with its whole history to its next owner with a transfer code — a real move, not a copy.
  • Import from Fuelio, or any app's CSV with the columns mapped by hand. Export to CSV and PDF, back up to JSON, and drive it all from a read-only JSON API and webhooks.

Free. No ads, no subscription, no locked features.

No tracking, no analytics, no profiles. Your records are yours: export everything whenever you like, and deleting your account takes it all with it.

Android and web, in English and Croatian.
