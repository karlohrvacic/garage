Scope: can the generic presets (oil, filters, plugs, brake fluid, coolant, timing belt, gearbox oil, fuel filter, glow plugs, serpentine belt, DPF, AdBlue) be made make-specific from public guidance, and is there a dataset/API worth using for EU cars?

Reading note: every getgaragehub page for the 18 makes was fetched and read in full; the saved first fetch (Škoda, Renault, auto-abc Duster, vehicledatabases, torquenode, automania, eurocarclinic) was read in full. ## 4. Recommendation

**A per-make table is defensible only for a narrow set of fields, and even those need a "typical — check your service book" label.**

Fields where a per-make default is honest:
- **Oil service regime** — but as *regime*, not one number: VAG (Škoda/VW/Seat/Cupra/Audi) fixed 15,000 km / 1 yr vs LongLife 30,000 km / 2 yr; Stellantis (Opel/Peugeot/Citroën) 25,000 km / 1 yr; Mercedes 25,000 km / 1 yr; Mazda 20,000 km / 1 yr; Toyota/Kia/Hyundai/Suzuki/Dacia/Fiat 15,000 km / 1 yr; Renault 30,000 km / 2 yr on TCe vs 15,000–20,000 on diesel; BMW and Ford condition-based (~2 yr). The safe default is the *shorter* regime, with the long-life one as the editable alternative.
- **Brake fluid** — 2 years for almost every make (Kia and Nissan: 2 yr / 30,000 km; Mazda 2 yr / 40,000 km). This is effectively a universal default, so make-specificity buys nothing.
- **Cabin filter** — 1–2 years everywhere; per-make only distinguishes annual (Peugeot/Citroën, Nissan 2 yr) from 2-yearly (VAG, Kia, Mazda, Mercedes).
- **Coolant** — genuinely varies by make and is time-based: ~5 yr (Stellantis, Renault, Dacia, Fiat), 8 yr (Toyota, Hyundai/Kia per auto-abc), 10 yr first fill (Kia 210,000 km, Mazda 200,000 km, VW G13). Good candidate for per-make.
- **Air filter** — roughly 30,000–60,000 km; per-make is fine as "typical".
- **Spark plugs** — per-make is marginal: values run from 30,000 km (Suzuki nickel, Dacia LPG) to 120,000 km (Mazda Skyactiv-G, Toyota US), and turbo vs NA vs iridium vs nickel inside one make swings 2–4×. Default per make only with a wide band, or key it on turbo/NA.

Fields that vary too much *by engine* to be per-make at all:
- **Timing belt/chain** — the worst case. Within one make and even one model year you get chain, dry belt and wet belt with intervals from 100,000 km / 6 yr (PureTech, revised) to 240,000 km (Kia D1.6) to "lifetime" (chains), and official figures have been revised downward after the fact (PureTech 175k→100k, Ford EcoBoost 150k mi→100k mi, Ford EcoBlue 240k→160k km). A make default here would be wrong for a large share of that make's fleet and is safety-relevant. This must be an engine-level attribute (belt / chain / belt-in-oil) captured on the vehicle, with the preset offered only once the type is known, and always with "years" as a co-equal limit.
- **Fuel filter** — petrol vs diesel first (petrol filters are mostly "maintenance-free" or 60,000–90,000 km), then 30,000 km (Dacia K9K) vs 40,000 km (PSA BlueHDi) vs 60,000 km (Kia, Mazda) vs 18,000 mi (Nissan). Key on fuel type, then make.
- **Gearbox oil** — depends on gearbox type, not make: manual (sealed or 45,000–180,000 km), torque-converter auto (60,000 km advisory vs "lifetime"), dry DSG/DCT (sealed), wet DSG/DCT (60,000 km / 40,000 hard use), CVT (60,000 km), Haldex/4x4 coupling (60,000 km / 3 yr). Key on transmission/drive type.
- **Oil interval within a make** — petrol vs diesel vs turbo vs long-life regime; the make default only works if the user picks the regime.
- **Glow plugs, DPF, AdBlue, serpentine belt** — no make-level guidance found at all; DPF/AdBlue are condition/consumption-based, not intervals.

Practical shape: keep the generic presets, add a **small per-make overlay for oil regime, coolant, cabin filter, air filter (and optionally plugs with a band)** built by hand from the manufacturer sources above for the Croatian top ~12 (which covers ~80 % of 2025 registrations), and make **fuel type, timing-drive type and transmission type** vehicle attributes that select the belt/fuel-filter/gearbox presets. Every overlay value needs a source link in the repo and a visible "typical for this make — confirm in your service book" caveat, because for the majority of these makes the only EU-official figures found came from a single model's owner's manual (Kia Ceed, Mazda CX-30, Nissan Juke), not a make-wide schedule.

Do not buy or scrape a dataset for this: neither API covers EU-market schedules, and auto-abc.eu is unlicensed and inconsistent with official figures in several spot checks.
